Automatic steps and your privacy

Daily Intake Calculator reads your daily step total from Health Connect only after you grant access. It uses steps to estimate walking calories and your daily calorie expenditure.

A recording app, such as Google Fit or Samsung Health, must share steps with Health Connect. This app does not record motion itself. You can choose Samsung Health records or Health Connect’s combined total. It refreshes steps while open and when you return to it.

Step totals are stored on your device. They are not sent to our servers or to the app's food lookup or AI services. If you export a backup, it includes your saved step totals; you control where you share that file.

You can revoke step access at any time in Health Connect's app permissions. Previously saved totals remain in this app until you clear its data in Android settings. Manual step entry remains available without Health Connect.

The optional step-data check reads source app packages, daily totals, and watch/phone record metadata. Its report stays on your device unless you copy and share it. Raw records are not included in the report.
