Body stats & steps

Used to estimate how many calories you burn per day (TDEE). Stored only on this phone.

Gym calories are estimated above resting expenditure using MET values (2.5 / 3.5 / 5.0), your weight and workout duration. Don't include walking already counted as steps.

How it's estimated: Mifflin–St Jeor resting calories × 1.2 provides the baseline. The estimated net walking cost of every recorded step is then added, starting from the first step. Walking distance is estimated from your height (step length = 0.413 × height) and walking cost from your weight (0.5 kcal per kg per km). If no step data is available, no step calories are added. Other exercise isn't counted.
