Body stats & steps

Used to estimate how many calories you burn per day (TDEE). Stored only on this phone.

How it's calculated (deliberately on the low side): Mifflin–St Jeor resting calories × 1.2, the lowest standard activity factor. Only steps beyond 5,000 a day are added, each at the net energy cost of walking (0.5 kcal per kg per km, from the ACSM walking equation) using a step length of 0.413 × your height. Gym workouts and other exercise aren't counted.
